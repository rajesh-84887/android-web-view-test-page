UPI Chooser Demo

Tap a button to open a UPI payment deeplink. On Android devices with UPI apps installed, the system should show a chooser of supported apps.

Available chooser links

Tap any of these if you want to open a UPI chooser directly from the link.

Note: Most Android browsers or WebViews will show an app chooser when multiple UPI apps are installed. The intent URI uses the Android intent format with scheme=upi and action=VIEW.

Note: This page works on Android devices and in Android WebViews / browsers that support UPI deeplinks. If no UPI app is installed, the browser may show an error or do nothing.
